Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026

Home-Tech Services (“Home-Tech”, “we”, “us”) provides home repair, maintenance and related services. This policy explains what information the website and service collect, how it is used, and how to contact us about it.

Information we collect

  • Account and sign-in: name, phone number and, where supplied, email address and account credentials. Firebase Authentication processes phone verification and Google sign-in. We receive sign-in verification from Firebase; we do not receive or store your Google password.
  • Bookings: selected service, appointment details, service address, contact details, booking status, quote and payment status. If you choose to share your location for a booking, coordinates may be shared with the assigned partner so they can locate the service address or provide job progress.
  • Partner applications: contact/profile details, city, service area, expertise, experience, address, application photo and (if requested) only the last four Aadhaar digits. Do not submit a full Aadhaar number or unrelated sensitive documents.
  • Operational data: support messages, timestamps, device/browser information needed for security and service operation, and payment references provided while resolving a UPI transfer. Home-Tech does not ask for your UPI PIN, card PIN or banking password.
  • Media: service/gallery images or videos uploaded by authorized staff and partner profile photos may be stored in Firebase Storage. Current application uploads are limited to 50 MB per file. Customer booking-photo/video upload is not currently offered through the booking form.

How we use information

We use it to create and secure accounts, prepare and manage bookings, calculate and collect the displayed charges, connect customers with the partner assigned to a job, provide support, review partner applications, prevent fraud, maintain records, and improve service reliability.

Who receives information

Booking contact, address, service and necessary job details are made available to the Home-Tech staff handling the booking and to the partner assigned to that booking. We use service providers for hosting, database, authentication, cloud media storage and payment processing, including Render, Netlify, Firebase/Google and Cloudinary. They process data to provide their services under their own terms. We do not publish customer or partner application details publicly.

Location and choices

Browser location access is requested only when you choose a location-dependent booking or tracking feature. You can deny or later revoke location permission in your browser; some address or tracking features may then be unavailable. Partner live-location updates are tied to assigned work and the partner’s use of the location feature.

Retention and security

We retain records while needed to operate bookings, accounts, partner relationships, resolve disputes, meet legal/accounting obligations and protect the service. Retention periods depend on the record and applicable requirements. We use access controls and service-provider security features, but no online service can promise absolute security.

Your requests

You may ask us to correct account information, raise a privacy concern, or request deletion where retention is not required. We may need to verify your identity and retain limited records where law, payment reconciliation or an unresolved dispute requires it. Contact hometakeservicesgroup@gmail.com or +91 92635 33988.

Children and changes

The service is intended for adults arranging services for a home or business. A parent/guardian should contact us if a child’s personal information has been submitted. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
