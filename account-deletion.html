Home-Tech   Privacy Policy

Request account deletion

Use this page to request deletion of your Home-Tech customer or partner account and associated personal data where retention is not required.

Send your request

Email us from the email address registered on your account. Include your full name, registered email address, and the words “Account deletion request”. Do not send passwords, payment PINs, or identity documents by email.

Email account deletion request

What happens next

  1. We verify that the request comes from the account holder.
  2. We delete or de-identify account data that is no longer needed.
  3. Some records may be retained where required for completed bookings, payment reconciliation, legal obligations, fraud prevention, or unresolved disputes.
