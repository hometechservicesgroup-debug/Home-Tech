Home-Tech Partner Terms

Effective date: 3 October 2026

These terms apply when you apply to join or use the Home-Tech partner dashboard. By submitting an application, you confirm that the details you provide are accurate and agree to these terms and the Privacy Policy.

Application, approval and account

  • Submitting an application does not guarantee approval, partner status or work. Home-Tech reviews applications and may ask for clarification or reject an incomplete or unverifiable application.
  • Keep your profile, service expertise, service area and contact details accurate and current. Protect your account, password and verification codes; do not share dashboard access.
  • Provide only the information requested. If Aadhaar digits are requested, enter only the last four digits. Never enter or upload your full Aadhaar number here. Application photos and profile details are used for review and administration and are not displayed publicly.

Job access and customer information

  • The dashboard is intended to show only jobs assigned to your partner account. Do not access, copy, disclose or use customer information for any purpose outside the assigned service.
  • Use customer contact/address details only to arrange and perform the assigned work. Do not share them with others, use them for marketing, or solicit off-platform payments or repeat business without Home-Tech’s permission.
  • Update job status accurately. Share location only for an active assigned job and only to support arrival/progress updates. Stop sharing when the job is no longer active.
  • Contact the customer respectfully, arrive within the agreed time where possible, explain any additional work/price and obtain customer approval before proceeding.

Wallet balance and commission

The current system requires a wallet balance of at least ₹500 for job eligibility. When a job is assigned, the current commission schedule deducts from the partner wallet based on the customer booking total:

  • ₹1–₹1,999: 10%
  • ₹2,000–₹4,999: 15%
  • ₹5,000 and above: 20%

The dashboard displays the applicable commission and wallet balance. Review them before accepting/being assigned work. Assignment is subject to approval, job availability, service area, expertise and sufficient wallet funds; work volume and earnings are not guaranteed. Wallet top-ups are processed through PhonePe when available. Contact Home-Tech promptly about a debit or top-up discrepancy.

Professional conduct and safety

  • Perform only work within your skills and the agreed scope. Follow applicable safety requirements, use appropriate tools, and stop if the job is unsafe or requires authorization you do not have.
  • Do not make false claims, damage property intentionally, harass or discriminate, bring unauthorized people to a job, or request a customer’s banking PIN, payment OTP or account password.
  • Protect customer property and confidential information. Report accidents, complaints, suspected fraud, data exposure or safety issues to Home-Tech promptly.
  • Do not upload unlawful, deceptive or third-party material without permission. Keep any photo/video of a customer’s premises private and use it only when necessary for the assigned service and with the customer’s permission.

Suspension and ending participation

Home-Tech may pause job access while reviewing safety, identity, customer complaints, suspected misuse or policy breaches, and may end partner access for serious or repeated breaches, subject to applicable law. You may ask to close your partner account by contacting us. Any unresolved wallet/payment records may need to be retained or reconciled as required by law and the payment provider.
